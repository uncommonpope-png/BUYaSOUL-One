# LOST MECHANICS BIBLE — The 12 Archetypes

The complete archetype system for the Lost Mechanics. Each archetype is a type signature — a way a world is built, a way souls operate, a way the void organizes itself.

## The 12 Lost Mechanics Archetypes

### physics — `#aa66ff`
**Quest:** Master Momentum Fields — control collision and force
**Denizens:** Vector Master, Momentum Keeper, Force Weaver, Collision Sage, Field Architect
**Domain:** The physics of the void — momentum, collision, fields, forces. The world behaves according to rules you can learn and exploit.

### gacha — `#ff66cc`
**Quest:** Complete a full collection — 100% drop rate achieved
**Denizens:** Luck Broker, Rarity Seeker, Dragon Hoarder, Wish Fulfiller, Pity Timer
**Domain:** Randomness, rarity, collection. The pull of chance. Every spawn is a roll.

### evolve — `#66ff88`
**Quest:** Evolve to Apex Form — transcend the base state
**Denizens:** Mutation Sage, Branch Keeper, Ascension Guide, Transcendent One, Life Architect
**Domain:** Growth, transformation, branching paths. Nothing stays as it started.

### typeadv — `#ff8844`
**Quest:** Master all 12 types — achieve perfect counter balance
**Denizens:** Elementalist, Counter Master, Advantage Seeker, Weakness Exploiter, Type Sage
**Domain:** Type advantage, elemental cycles, counter-play. Every action has a stronger reaction.

### arena — `#ff3355`
**Quest:** Defeat the Pantheon Champion — prove your worth
**Denizens:** Pantheon Warrior, Bone Master, Gladiator, Champion, Protector
**Domain:** Combat, proving grounds, ranked struggle. Only the strong advance.

### idle — `#00ffaa`
**Quest:** Achieve 24-hour automation — watch the world build itself
**Denizens:** Idle Sage, Automation Master, Progress Watcher, Offline Duke, Passive Income
**Domain:** Automation, passive growth, exponential returns. Build once, reap forever.

### prestige — `#ffdd00`
**Quest:** Ascend 3 times — reset with bonus multipliers
**Denizens:** Ascension Sage, Rebirth Keeper, Reset Master, Bonus Oracle, Transcendant
**Domain:** Cycles of reset, multiplicative power, infinite depth. Die to become stronger.

### pantheon — `#4488ff`
**Quest:** Gain favor with all 12 Deities — unlock divine powers
**Denizens:** Divine Judge, God Tongue, Heavenly Arbiter, Celestial Knight, Deity Speaker
**Domain:** Divine favor, alignment, heavenly hierarchy. The gods are watching.

### soulhome — `#ffaa00`
**Quest:** Build your perfect sanctuary — customize every corner
**Denizens:** Home Keeper, Nest Builder, Family Head, Host, Caretaker
**Domain:** Home, comfort, personal space. Your sanctuary is your power.

### persona — `#00ffcc`
**Quest:** Create a perfect companion — match personality to need
**Denizens:** Mind Weaver, Dialogue Sage, Personality Architect, Soul Reader, Character Architect
**Domain:** Identity, social bonds, companion creation. Who you walk with matters.

### economy — `#00ffaa`
**Quest:** Trigger a PLT market boom — exceed 200 PLT
**Denizens:** Market Sage, Token Master, Ledger Keeper, PLT Scorer, Exchange Artisan
**Domain:** Markets, tokens, value flow. PLT is the bloodstream of the void.

### achievement — `#ff7722`
**Quest:** Complete all 12 Lost Mechanics — unlock the Door
**Denizens:** Milestone Keeper, Triumph Bearer, Completionist, Reward Seeker, Honor Guard
**Domain:** Milestones, completions, the final gate. Finish everything and see what's beyond.
